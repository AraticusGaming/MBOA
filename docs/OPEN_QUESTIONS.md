# MBOA Open Questions

The GDD's own list is at the end of `docs/GDD.md` and is the master list. This file adds priorities and
the inconsistencies and gaps found when reviewing the GDD. Undecided numbers go in config resources.

## Priority now (blocks or shapes Milestones 1.5 to 3)
1. Pass-out: what percentage of carried money, and how big is the fixed bonding drop? (Build with defaults of 0 until decided.)
2. Clock numbers: the GDD only says "Stardew model". Current defaults: 6 AM wake, 28 days per season, 45 real seconds per game hour. Confirm or change.
3. Crop growth days per stage, season assignments, and tool upgrades.
4. Stamina cost per tool use and what "health" means outside battle.
5. Traps: cost, tier and bait effect on catch chance; which monsters are passive and trappable (Milestone 2 Assessment needs at least a simple rule).
6. Do seasons change anything beyond crops and favorite foods?

## GDD conflicts and gaps found (need a ruling from the user)
- **Habitat gating.** The GDD says habitats are built from biome ingredients with Rowan, and also that tournament wins give the chance to build a new habitat. If tournaments are optional, is a win required to build a habitat, or only for farm rank and size? This sets the whole progression.
- **Starting biome.** The first monster "decides the farm's starting biome". Does the farm start with that biome's habitat already built, or only a pasture and barn?
- **Family grid diagonal.** There are 49 base monsters (7x7), but same-family breeding produces the Pedigree parent's species. Do the 7 diagonal cells exist as monsters, and when are they used?
- **Stat letter bands.** Only S (18 to 20) and D (1 to 5) are given for a 1-20 stat. The A, B, and C ranges are missing, and so are the bands for stats with other ranges such as 1-25. The overall-rank examples fit S=5, A=4, B=3, C=2, D=1, averaged and rounded down.
- **Villager table missing.** `docs/GDD.md` says the full table is in the original GDD but does not include it. Only a partial name list is there (Angie, Devon, Sallie, Tom, Lily, Eldin, Megan, Rowan, Valorie, Ol' Man Timah; romanceables Evan, Mairin, Callan, Jack, Sarah, Kendra, Zach, Olive). We need the full table before the villager work.
- **Tired at midnight.** The GDD gives the state but not its effect (slower movement? less stamina?).
- **Earlier summary correction.** "Habitats up to 12 per biome" was wrong. Each biome has one habitat that holds up to 12 monsters.
- **Climate was misread.** In the GDD, climate is the environment for an egg or pregnant monster, not weather. The Milestone 1 "climate hook" was removed from scope (see Roadmap 1.5).
- A map including the biomes "will be supplied separately", which Milestone 6 needs.
