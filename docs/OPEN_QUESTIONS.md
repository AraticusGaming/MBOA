# MBOA Open Questions

Tracked from the GDD summary (full GDD not yet in repo). Each should become a configurable constant or a data
value in `resources/` until decided. The GDD is the source of truth; update this file when it changes.

## Blocking Milestone 1 (use configurable constants)
- Climate and seasons beyond crop effects
- Pass-out penalty (percent of money or items lost, where the player wakes)
- Crop growth days (per crop)
- Mobile controls

## Milestone 2 (food, Assessment, traps)
- Trap costs and success chances (3 tiers, biome-specific)
- Tool upgrades
- Appraisal skill

## Milestone 3 (habitats, barn, bonding)
- Recovery timers
- Egg vs pregnant for breeding
- Portal costs

## Later
- Skills inheritance
- Seasons content beyond crops
- Reputation details (6 categories, guarded A/S areas)
- Romance and dating depth (8 romanceable adults, 20 named NPCs)

## Known design facts (from user summary, verify against GDD)
- Capacity: habitats up to 12 per biome, pasture 6, barn 20-100 with upgrades
- Bonding: 25% to use barn, 100% for breeding star
- Families: Bird, Reptile, Insect, Arachnid, Aquatic, Beast, Critter; Plant and Elemental are modifiers only
- Stat inheritance: Pedigree base plus letter bonuses from second parent (S+5, A+4, ...), bonuses not inherited
- Taming methods: Food, Battle, Trap
- Battles: 1-2 creatures, turn-based, auto or direct, wild recruit chance after defeat
