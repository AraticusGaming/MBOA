## Look
A small lop-eared forest rabbit with warm fawn fur, a cream belly and a fluffy white cottontail. A cushion of moss grows on its crown, with a two-leaf sprout. Its ear tips and the saddle on its back are mossy too. It has dark bead eyes and twitches its nose. It moves in short hops.

## Notes (placeholder)
- Shy grazer that sleeps in mossy hollows; the moss on its back helps it hide on the forest floor.
- Smallest of the three Woodland additions: the body is about 14 px wide, so it fits in one 16x16 tile.
