# Cinderkin

Test creature for the creature-loop slice. Concept image: `assets/reference/cinderkin_concept.jpg`.
Sources: GDD ("Monster ideas so far", "Breeding modifiers"), Founders Document (lore entry, Waxweed entry).

## From the GDD
- A chipmunk (Critter family) combined with the Fire modifier.
- Because Fire is a breeding modifier, Cinderkin is a modifier-result monster: it stays in the Critter family.
- Breeding a Cinderkin with another monster gives the plain base species, not a Cinderkin; with a different-family monster and no recipe it follows the family grid using the Critter family (example in the GDD: Cinderkin x Reptile gives the Critter and Reptile cell).

## From the Founders Document (lore)
- "A small flying creature resembling a sugar-glider and hedgehog hybrid."
- Feeds on **wet Waxweed**, converting the oil into **flammable spines** as a defense mechanism.
- Considered a pest and killed on sight by people.
- Exceptionally old specimens reportedly produce **blue flame** and are virtually mythical.
- Waxweed: a thick moss growing on trees and forest floors. Harmless when wet; once dried it makes an extremely flammable oil. Must be transported wet.

## Look (from the concept image)
Grey-brown fur with a cream chest and muzzle, huge round amber eyes, large pointed ears with orange inner glow, gliding membranes (wings) lit orange at the edges, a big flame-like tail, small flame crest on the forehead. Cozy and cute, not menacing.
